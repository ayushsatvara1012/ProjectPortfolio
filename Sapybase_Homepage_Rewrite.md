## Copy Tone Guidelines for Sapybase

**What TO Do:**
- Use "you" language: "You get," "You save," "Your AI"
- Lead with outcomes, not features
- Use short sentences (8-12 words)
- Use conversational language: "Let's be real," "No BS," "That's it"
- Use numbers and metrics
- Use examples: "like Shopify, WordPress, Webflow"
- Be honest: "When it doesn't know something, it says so"

**What NOT to Do:**
- Don't say "RAG pipeline," "vectorization," "Gemini models" (too technical for homepage)
- Don't use jargon: "AI agent," "autonomous chatbot," "LLM"
- Don't make claims you can't back up
- Don't sound corporate: "innovative solutions," "cutting-edge platform"
- Don't bury the benefits in technical details
