# Sapybase Vector Illustration Strategy
**Style:** Minimal, Claude-inspired (clean, geometric, self-explanatory)  
**Colors:** Blue (#4f46e5 primary) + Green (#10b981 accent) + White/Gray neutrals  
**Goal:** Visually explain the concept WITHOUT needing to read text

---

## Illustration Overview

You're creating 4 key illustrations for the homepage:
1. **Hero Section** — Problem/Solution contrast
2. **Speed Section** — 10 minutes vs 2 weeks
3. **Customize Section** — Works with everything
4. **ROI Section** — Cost savings + impact

Each illustration should work independently—someone should understand the concept just by looking at the image.

---

## ILLUSTRATION 1: Hero Section
**"Custom AI That Actually Understands Your Business"**

### What It Should Show
**Split-screen comparison:**
- **Left side (Problem):** Generic AI confusion
- **Right side (Solution):** Sapybase understanding

### Visual Concept

**LEFT SIDE — Generic AI (Getting It Wrong):**
- A person asks a question (speech bubble)
- An AI head/brain with confused symbols: ? ? ? 
- Around it: floating words showing generic, irrelevant knowledge (random icons: earth, cloud, telescope)
- AI responds with confused/wrong answer (red X or confused emoji)
- Customer frustrated (confused face)

**Arrow or transition in the middle**

**RIGHT SIDE — Sapybase AI (Getting It Right):**
- Same person asks same question
- An AI head/brain with focused symbols
- Around it: only YOUR knowledge (documents, databases, FAQ icons) — shown in blue/green
- AI responds with checkmark (green ✓)
- Customer happy (satisfied face)

### Color Scheme
- **Left side:** Grays + Red accent (for error/confusion)
- **Right side:** Blue + Green (for correctness/success)
- White backgrounds

### Key Visual Elements
- Question mark icons
- Checkmark icons
- Document/knowledge icons
- Happy/sad faces (minimal style)
- Simple geometric brain shape

### Size
- ~600px wide × 300px tall
- Works as a wide banner above the hero text

### Why This Works
Without reading any text, the user immediately sees: "This AI understands your knowledge. Generic AI doesn't."

## Design Guidelines for All Illustrations

### Style (Claude-inspired)
- ✅ Clean, geometric shapes
- ✅ Minimal line weights (2-3px strokes)
- ✅ Limited color palette (primarily blue + green + white/gray)
- ✅ Simple icons and symbols
- ✅ Generous white space
- ✅ No gradients, no shadows, no complex details
- ✅ SVG format (scalable, lightweight)

### Color Palette
- **Primary Blue:** #4f46e5 (Sapybase brand)
- **Accent Green:** #10b981 (success/positive)
- **Background:** White (#ffffff)
- **Text/Outlines:** Dark gray (#374151)
- **Disabled/Muted:** Light gray (#d1d5db)
- **Error/Problem:** #ef4444 (red, used sparingly)

### Icon Style
- 2px stroke weight for most icons
- 24px × 24px base size (scales up easily)
- Minimal detail (2-3 elements per icon)
- Consistent line endings (rounded or squared, not mixed)

### Typography (if needed)
- Numbers only (no words in illustrations)
- Large, bold, sans-serif
- Blue or green color
- 48-72px size minimum

### Grid & Alignment
- Use 4px or 8px grid for precise alignment
- Center illustrations on the page
- Leave generous margins (at least 20% white space around edge)

## Illustration Production Checklist

### For each illustration:
- [ ] Create in Figma or Adobe Illustrator
- [ ] Export as SVG (clean code, no fill="#000000" styles)
- [ ] Export as PNG (2x resolution for Retina displays)
- [ ] Test at multiple sizes (mobile 300px, desktop 600px, large 900px)
- [ ] Verify colors match brand palette
- [ ] Test accessibility (good contrast, not color-only conveying meaning)
- [ ] Get feedback: "Can someone understand this without reading text?"

## Integration on Homepage

### Hero Illustration
- **Placement:** Right side of hero text (desktop) or below text (mobile)
- **Width:** ~50% of container on desktop
- **Aspect ratio:** 2:1 (wider than tall)

### Speed Illustration
- **Placement:** Centered in "Speed" section, above the body text
- **Width:** ~70% of container (full width on mobile)
- **Aspect ratio:** Roughly square or slightly wider

### Customize Illustration
- **Placement:** Centered in "Customize" section
- **Width:** ~60% of container
- **Aspect ratio:** Square or tall

### ROI Illustration
- **Placement:** Centered in "ROI" section, above metrics
- **Width:** ~70% of container
- **Aspect ratio:** 2:1 (wider than tall)

## Success Metrics

How to know if an illustration is working:
- ✅ A complete stranger can understand the concept in 3 seconds
- ✅ The illustration matches your minimal, friendly brand
- ✅ The colors are correct (blue + green)
