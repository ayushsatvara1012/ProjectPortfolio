# Sapybase: 30-Day Marketing Sprint
**Goal:** Get 5-10 qualified leads / 1-2 paying customers in next 30 days  
**Budget:** $0-500 (organic focus)  
**Status:** MVP/Beta with limited customers

---

## 🚀 WEEK 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)

### PRIORITY 1: Ship the New Homepage (Days 1-3)
**Why:** Everything starts here. You have the copy, illustrations, and strategy ready.

**Action:**
1. [ ] Deploy new homepage with:
   - New headline: "Custom AI That Actually Understands Your Business"
   - 3 value props (Speed, Knowledge, Customization)
   - ROI section with real numbers ($15K-40K savings)
   - CTA: "Start Building for Free" (no credit card)
   - Trust badge: "14-day free trial. No credit card required."

2. [ ] Set up simple analytics:
   - Google Analytics on homepage (track visitors, time on page, CTA clicks)
   - Hotjar or Clarity (heatmaps to see where people click)
   - Form submission tracking (capture emails)

**Time:** 3-4 hours (if dev-ready) or 1 day (with developer)

**Outcome:** Live funnel to capture leads

---

### PRIORITY 2: Extract Testimonials from Current Customers (Days 2-4)
**Why:** Social proof is your #1 conversion lever. You likely have 3-5 beta users.

**Action:**
1. [ ] Email your current users:
```
Subject: "I need your honest feedback (takes 2 min)"

Hi [Name],

We're launching new messaging for Sapybase. Would you mind answering 3 quick questions?

1. Before Sapybase, what was your biggest support headache?
2. After using it, what surprised you most?
3. Would you recommend Sapybase to a friend? (Yes/No + why)

Your answer might become a testimonial on our site. I'll make you look great 😊

Thanks!
[Your name]
```

2. [ ] Capture any video testimonials (even 30 seconds on WhatsApp/Loom):
   - "Here's how much time Sapybase saved us..."
   - "We went from X to Y within 2 weeks..."

**Time:** 2 hours outreach + waiting for responses

**Outcome:** 2-3 testimonials to feature on homepage + landing pages

---

### PRIORITY 3: Set Up Lead Capture (Days 3-5)
**Why:** You need an email list to nurture leads.

**Action:**
1. [ ] Create simple lead magnet (choose ONE):
   - **"ROI Calculator"** — "See how much you could save" (link to Figma prototype or simple form)
   - **"5-day email course"** — "How to cut support costs 60%" (automated sequence)
   - **"Checklist"** — "12-point support audit" (PDF)

2. [ ] Set up email capture:
   - Segment A: "Try free for 14 days" (product trial)
   - Segment B: "See ROI calculator" (research phase)
   - Auto-follow-up sequence (see below)

3. [ ] Tools (free tier):
   - **Email:** ConvertKit, Mailchimp, or Brevo (all free for <500 subscribers)
   - **Form:** Typeform, Google Forms, or native HTML form
   - **Landing page:** Carrd.co ($19/year) or just use homepage

**Time:** 2-3 hours setup

**Outcome:** Email list started, automated nurture sequence running

```
Subject: "Quick question about your customer support"

Hi [Name],

support emails about common questions.

$15-40K/year by reducing support team workload.
```

3. [ ] Track responses:

**Outcome:** 3-5 qualified demos scheduled

### PRIORITY 7: One Blog Post / Case Study (Days 18-21)
**Why:** SEO traffic + social proof + something to share.

**Pick ONE:**

**Option A: Blog Post** (1000 words, 3-4 hours)
- Title: "Why Generic Chatbots Fail (And How to Fix It)"
- SEO keywords: "chatbot for customer support," "AI support," "customer service automation"
- Share on LinkedIn, Reddit (r/entrepreneur), HackerNews

**Option B: Customer Case Study** (800 words, 2-3 hours)
- Format: [Customer story, numbers, what they learned]
- Example: "How [Company] Cut Support Costs 60% in 30 Days with Sapybase"
- Share with prospective customers during outreach

**Time:** 3-4 hours research + writing

**Outcome:** Shareable content, SEO juice (in 3 months), case study for demos

## 💰 WEEK 4: Conversion & Optimization (Days 22-30)

### PRIORITY 8: Sales Follow-up + Demos (Days 22-27)
**Action:**
1. [ ] Schedule demos with interested leads
2. [ ] Use proven demo flow:
   - **Problem:** "What's your biggest support challenge?"
   - **Solution:** "Here's how Sapybase handles it"
   - **Proof:** Show your ROI calculator
   - **CTA:** "Want to try it free?"

3. [ ] Ask for referrals (at every touchpoint):
   - "Know another founder struggling with support?"
   - Offer small reward (discount, feature request priority)

**Time:** 2-3 hours (depends on demo requests)

**Outcome:** 1-2 paying customers (goal)

---

### PRIORITY 9: Optimize Homepage (Days 25-30)
**Action:**
1. [ ] Check analytics:
   - Where do people drop off?
   - Which CTA gets most clicks?
   - Time on page?

2. [ ] Quick optimizations:
   - A/B test headline (test 2 versions)
   - A/B test CTA button color (blue vs green)
   - Move social proof higher on page if bounce rate is high
   - Add urgency: "14-day free trial. No credit card. 5 min to set up."

3. [ ] User feedback:
   - Ask 3-5 users: "What confused you about this page?"
   - Iterate based on feedback

**Time:** 2-3 hours

**Outcome:** 10-20% higher conversion rate

## What NOT to Do (Yet)

❌ Don't pay for ads (organic is faster at MVP stage)  
❌ Don't build complex features (focus on selling what you have)  
❌ Don't wait for "perfect" (launch with 80% ready)  
❌ Don't build features nobody asked for (sell first, then build)  
❌ Don't spend on agencies/consultants (bootstrapped stage)
