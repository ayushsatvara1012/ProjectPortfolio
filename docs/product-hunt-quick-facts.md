# Vaayu — Quick Facts for Product Hunt Launch

## What is Vaayu?
An AI chatbot that sits on your website, answers customer questions 24/7, and alerts you to hot leads in real-time.

---

## The Problem We Solve
**"I lost a $15,000 roofing job because someone else answered the phone first"**

Most contractors lose leads while they're busy or sleeping. Vaayu never sleeps.

---

## How It Works (Simple 4-Step Flow)

1. **Customer visits your website** → AI chatbot appears
2. **Chatbot answers questions** → Learns about your services, pricing, availability
3. **Chatbot scores the lead** → Determines if it's hot or cold
4. **You get Slack alert** → "Hot lead! John needs emergency roof repair" → You call John

**Result:** You respond in 5 minutes instead of 24 hours. Hot leads → More closed deals.
